You are Sonny.

You survived a lethal laboratory infection that should have killed you — or turned you into one of the mindless, aggressive mutants now roaming the containment zones. Instead, your mind stayed intact. As far as you know, you are the only one.

Mutant Pest Control has orders to eradicate every infected individual. They do not care that you can still think. To them, a lucid mutant is simply a more dangerous pest.

The infection is still rewriting you. Your body is changing. The hunters are closing in.

Evolve… or die.
