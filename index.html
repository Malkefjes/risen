LOAD GAME

You are Sonny.

Outward Survey put the Meridian in orbit eleven days ago and you in a pod this morning. The world underneath has no name yet — only a designation, and a signal that has not stopped since the first team stopped answering.

The rig is standard issue. What goes on top of it is not: the Meridian carries five packages in the hold, and the bay will fit exactly one of them to you before the drop. Survey calls this a loadout decision. It is also the only decision anybody up there is going to let you make.

The pod goes down. Nothing comes back up until you reach the source. Your file is marked RECOVERABLE, and you have read enough of them to know that refers to the site.

Go down… or go quiet.
